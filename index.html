Welcome to MA AJAB TOURS AND SAFARIS

Creating Memories in the Heart of Wild Kenya

MA AJAB TOURS AND SAFARIS is a fully registered travel agency based in Watamu, Kenya with over 10 years of experience in the tourism industry. Our professional team has come together to create a reliable and passionate travel company dedicated to delivering unforgettable adventures across Kenya.

From the white sandy beaches of the coast to the magical savannahs of Kenya’s most famous parks and reserves, we create experiences that combine comfort, adventure, wildlife, and culture.

Whether you are looking for a short safari, a luxury wildlife experience, airport transfers, excursions, or accommodation assistance, our team is ready to make your journey smooth, exciting, and memorable.
