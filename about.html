Our Background

A Decade of Guiding Magical Adventures

MA AJAB TOURS AND SAFARIS is a trusted travel agency based in Watamu, Kenya with more than 10 years of experience in tourism and safari operations.

Our company was built on passion, professionalism, and the desire to share the beauty of Kenya with travelers from around the world. Our experienced team members specialize in safari planning, guest services, transfers, excursions, and customized adventures.

With our strategic location in Watamu, we are uniquely positioned to offer seamless experiences that combine Kenya’s spectacular coastline with the country’s iconic wildlife destinations.

We believe every traveler deserves a smooth, safe, exciting, and unforgettable journey. Let us guide you through the raw magic of the African wild.

Our Foundations

Our Core Values

The driving philosophies behind our impeccable safety record and outstanding customer relationships.

Authenticity

We pride ourselves on hiring local guides and spotters who grew up on the land, connecting you deeply to true Swahili culture and native wildlife tracks.

Sustainable Eco-Tourism

We strictly follow wildlife protection codes, support local communities directly, and support eco-friendly camps that respect delicate park ecosystems.

Complete Trust & Safety

Every transit vehicle is completely insured and regularly serviced, equipped with long-range radio tracking, and operated by seasoned professional guides.
